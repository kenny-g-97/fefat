## Context

The problem and the forces behind the decision: what was true, what was needed, and what it cost.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two short paragraphs.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: what it makes easy, what it rules out, and where the plan or the code carries it.

## How we would know it is wrong

The observation that would show the decision no longer holds, stated so that someone can check it.
